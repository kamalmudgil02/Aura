Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters. Here's how we handle your data.

Effective Date: January 1, 2026  |  Last Updated: March 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you visit our store or make a purchase, we collect the following types of information:

  • Personal Information: Name, email address, shipping address, phone number, and payment information when you make a purchase.
  • Device Information: Browser type, IP address, time zone, and cookies for site functionality.
  • Order Information: Products purchased, order value, and transaction details.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Communicate with you about your order (confirmations, tracking, updates)
  • Send marketing communications (only with your consent — you can opt out anytime)
  • Improve our website, products, and customer experience
  • Prevent fraud and secure transactions

3. Data Sharing

We never sell your personal data. We share information only with:

  • Payment processors (Stripe, PayPal) to process transactions securely
  • Shipping carriers (USPS, FedEx, DHL) to deliver your orders
  • Analytics tools (anonymized data only) to improve our service

4. Cookies

We use cookies to remember your cart, preferences, and provide a seamless shopping experience. You can control cookie settings in your browser.

5. Data Security

All transactions are encrypted using SSL/TLS technology. Payment information is processed through PCI-compliant payment processors and is never stored on our servers.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your personal data
  • Opt out of marketing communications at any time
  • Request a copy of your data
  • File a complaint with a data protection authority

7. Children's Privacy

Our store is not intended for children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

9. Contact Us

For privacy-related questions, contact us at:

  • Email: privacy@aura.com
  • Or visit our Contact Page
