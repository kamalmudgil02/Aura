Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shopping with Aura.

📦 Orders & Shipping

How long does shipping take? ▾

Standard shipping takes 5–7 business days within the US. Express shipping (2–3 business days) is available at checkout for an additional fee. International orders take 10–14 business days.

Is shipping free? ▾

Yes! We offer free standard shipping on all US orders over $150. Orders under $150 ship for a flat rate of $5.99.

How do I track my order? ▾

Once your order ships, you'll receive a tracking number via email. You can also track your order on our Track Order page using your Order ID and email.

Do you ship internationally? ▾

Yes! We ship to over 50 countries. International shipping rates and delivery times vary by destination. Customs duties may apply.

🔄 Returns & Exchanges

What is your return policy? ▾

We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy. Items must be unworn, unwashed, and in their original packaging. Visit our Returns & Exchange page to start a return.

Can I exchange an item? ▾

Absolutely! If you need a different size or color, initiate an exchange through our Returns & Exchange page. We'll ship the new item as soon as we receive your return.

How long do refunds take? ▾

Once we receive your return, refunds are processed within 3–5 business days. It may take an additional 5–10 days for the refund to appear on your statement.

👕 Products

What materials do you use? ▾

We use responsibly sourced eucalyptus tree fiber, organic Pima cotton, and recycled polyester. All our materials are OEKO-TEX® certified, meaning they're free from harmful chemicals.

How do I find my size? ▾

Each product page includes a Size Guide link with detailed measurements. When in doubt, size up — our fabrics have a natural stretch. You can also contact us for personalized sizing help.

How should I care for my Aura products? ▾

Machine wash cold with like colors. Tumble dry low or hang dry for best results. Avoid bleach. Our fabrics are designed to get softer with every wash.

💳 Payment & Discounts

What payment methods do you accept? ▾

We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex), PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and Shop Pay. All transactions are encrypted and secure.

Do you offer discount codes? ▾

Yes! Sign up for our newsletter to get 10% off your first order. We also run seasonal promotions and exclusive member discounts.
